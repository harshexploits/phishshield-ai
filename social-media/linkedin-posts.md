🛡️ I built an AI-powered cybersecurity tool in one weekend — and here's what it does.

Every day, millions of people fall for phishing attacks. Fake emails. Suspicious links. QR codes that steal your data.

So I asked myself: What if AI could analyze ANY suspicious content in real-time?

That's how **PhishShield AI** was born.

🚀 **What it does:**
→ Paste a suspicious URL → AI tells you if it's safe, suspicious, or malicious
→ Upload a screenshot of a phishing email → AI analyzes it visually
→ Scan QR codes → Detects "quishing" attacks
→ Paste raw email headers → Checks SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication
→ Batch analyze up to 20 URLs at once

🎯 **What makes it different:**
→ 5 analysis modes (most tools only have 1)
→ Works in 13 languages
→ WHOIS domain age lookup
→ URL unshortener (reveals where bit.ly/tinyurl links actually go)
→ Plain-English explanations (not technical jargon)
→ Completely FREE — no signup, no credit card

💡 **Tech Stack:**
→ Python + Streamlit
→ Google Gemini AI
→ OpenCV (QR code detection)
→ Deployed on Render

🔗 Try it here: https://phishxrecon.onrender.com
